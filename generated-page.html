The Project

The CL
Paradigm

We observed a dissonance in modern interiors: objects were either starkly architectural but physically punishing, or comfortable yet visually formless.

Over three years of iterative prototyping, we collaborated with orthopedic specialists and master woodworkers. The result is a collection that acts as an extension of the human body.
